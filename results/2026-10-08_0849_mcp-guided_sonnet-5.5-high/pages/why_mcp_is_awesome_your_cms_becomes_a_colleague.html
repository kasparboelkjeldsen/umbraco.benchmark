Why MCP is awesome: your CMS becomes a colleague

With MCP, an assistant can model content, upload media and publish pages through the same safe tools an editor would use.

From chatbot to teammate

A chatbot can only talk about your website. An assistant with MCP access can actually work on it: inspect the content model, create a page, upload an image and publish the result.

That shift, from describing work to doing it, is what makes MCP such a big deal for content teams.

“The assistant doesn't get a back door. It gets the same front door as everyone else.”

— On permissions and MCP

Safe by design

Because every action goes through a named tool, you can see exactly what an assistant is allowed to do. Destructive steps can ask for confirmation, and editors keep the final say over what goes live.

That combination of real capability and clear guard rails is why MCP feels less like a gimmick and more like infrastructure.
