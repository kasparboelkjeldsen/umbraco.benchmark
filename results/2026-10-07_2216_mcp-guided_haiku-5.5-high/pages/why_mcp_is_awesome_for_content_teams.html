Why MCP is awesome for content teams

MCP lets an editor ask for a change in plain language, and lets the tool do exactly that and nothing more. Here is what that looks like on a real site.

Editors rarely want a new admin screen. They want to say what they need and see it done. MCP makes that possible by giving an assistant a well-defined set of actions on the site.

What editors get

A plain-language request, a clear result, and a confirmation before anything is published.

Start small: one page, one change, one review. Once the pattern is clear, the same approach scales across the whole site.
