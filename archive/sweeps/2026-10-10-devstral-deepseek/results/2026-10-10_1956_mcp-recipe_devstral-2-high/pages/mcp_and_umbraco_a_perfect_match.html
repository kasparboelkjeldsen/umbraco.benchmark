MCP and Umbraco: A Perfect Match

Published: October 10, 2026

Umbraco is a powerful and flexible CMS, and MCP servers make it even more powerful. Together, they create a seamless experience for content editors and developers alike.

With the Umbraco MCP server configured, you can manage your entire content structure through Claude Code. Create pages, upload media, organize document types - all through natural language commands.

This integration means you spend less time clicking through admin interfaces and more time building great content experiences.

The combination of Umbraco's flexibility and MCP's integration capabilities is a game-changer for content management.
