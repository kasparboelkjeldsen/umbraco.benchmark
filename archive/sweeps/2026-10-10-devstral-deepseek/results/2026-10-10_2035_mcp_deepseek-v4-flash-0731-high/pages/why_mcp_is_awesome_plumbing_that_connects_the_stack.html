What it actually is

MCP — the Model Context Protocol — is an open standard that lets an AI model talk to tools the same way your browser talks to printers: through a protocol almost nobody needs to think about.

A server, a client, three verbs

Clients connect to servers. Servers expose three things:

  • Tools — functions the model can call, declared once, executed anywhere.
  • Resources — data the model can read before it starts answering.
  • Prompts — reusable instruction templates.

That is the whole spec. What makes it valuable is not the list — it is what the list implies: a model can now reach into your CMS, your database and your command line without anyone writing bespoke glue per app.
