MCP Servers: The Future of AI Integration

Published: October 01, 2026

MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers represent a significant leap forward in how AI agents can interact with content management systems like Umbraco.

Traditionally, integrating AI with CMS platforms required custom APIs, complex authentication flows, and significant development effort. MCP servers standardize this interaction, providing a consistent interface that AI agents can use to read and modify content.

With MCP servers, AI agents can:

  • Query content types, document types, and media
  • Create and update pages and media
  • Publish and unpublish content
  • Manage the entire content lifecycle

This standardization reduces the barrier to entry for AI integration and makes it possible for non-developers to leverage AI capabilities.

MCP is not just a protocol, it's a paradigm shift in how we think about AI and content management.
