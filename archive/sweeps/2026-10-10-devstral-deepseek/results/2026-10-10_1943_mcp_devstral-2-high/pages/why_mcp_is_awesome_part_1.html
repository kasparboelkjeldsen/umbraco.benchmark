Why MCP is Awesome - Part 1

Published: October 10, 2026

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is a game-changer for AI applications. It provides a standardized way for AI models to interact with external data sources, tools, and APIs.

In the context of Umbraco, MCP allows AI agents to seamlessly read and write content, manage media, and perform administrative tasks through a well-defined interface. This means you can automate content creation, updates, and management without writing custom integration code.

One of the most powerful aspects of MCP is its flexibility. Whether you're building a simple blog or a complex multi-site installation, MCP provides the tools you need to make AI a first-class citizen in your content workflow.
