Why MCP is Awesome - Part 2

Published: October 10, 2026

Building on the foundation of Part 1, let's dive deeper into what makes MCP truly special for Umbraco developers.

MCP isn't just about reading and writing content - it's about enabling a whole new class of applications. With MCP, you can build AI-powered content assistants, automated migration tools, and intelligent content analysis systems that understand your Umbraco structure.

The protocol's design emphasizes safety and predictability. Each operation is clearly defined, permissions are respected, and the AI always works within the bounds of what's allowed. This means you can trust MCP-powered tools to work correctly without unexpected side effects.

For developers, MCP reduces the barrier to entry for building powerful integrations. The standardized interface means you spend less time on plumbing and more time on creating value for your users.
