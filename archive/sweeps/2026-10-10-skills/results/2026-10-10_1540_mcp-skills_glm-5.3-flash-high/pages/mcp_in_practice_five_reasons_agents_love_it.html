MCP in Practice: Five Reasons Agents Love It

What the protocol looks like once it leaves the slide deck.

Theory is nice, but a protocol earns its reputation in deployment. Here are five reasons teams keep choosing MCP once they try it in anger.

1. One standard, many clients. Write your server once and every MCP-speaking agent can use it.

2. Composability. Tools, resources, and prompts compose cleanly, so agents can chain capabilities without bespoke glue code.

3. Security boundaries. Servers declare what they expose; clients mediate access, so permissions are explicit rather than implicit.

4. Ecosystem momentum. Thousands of community servers already exist, from databases to design tools.

5. It is boring on purpose. The spec stays small, which makes audits, tests, and long-term maintenance realistic.

“The best protocol is the one you stop noticing.”
