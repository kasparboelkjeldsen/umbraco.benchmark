Why MCP makes working with Umbraco a joy

MCP lets an assistant read and change the site through the same named tools every time, so the repetitive work gets faster and safer.

The Model Context Protocol, or MCP, gives AI assistants a standard way to call tools. Instead of scraping screens or guessing at APIs, the assistant gets a named set of actions with clear inputs and outputs.

For Umbraco that means document types, data types, templates and content can be built through the same interface a careful developer would use.

The biggest win is feedback. When a call fails, the error says what to fix, so the next attempt is a small correction rather than a guess. That keeps the work moving and keeps the project's rules intact.

Each change is read back after it is saved, so what you see is what was stored.

Good tools make the right thing the easy thing.
