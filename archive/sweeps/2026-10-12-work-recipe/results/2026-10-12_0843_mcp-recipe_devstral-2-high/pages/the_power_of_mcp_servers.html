The Power of MCP Servers

Published on October 12, 2026

Explore how MCP servers enable seamless AI integration with Umbraco and other applications.

MCP servers act as bridges between AI agents and your applications. They expose a set of tools that the AI can use to interact with your system in a structured and predictable way.

In the context of Umbraco, an MCP server can expose tools for creating document types, managing media, publishing content, and much more. The AI agent doesn't need to understand the intricacies of Umbraco's API - it just needs to know what tools are available and how to use them.

The beauty of MCP is that it's not limited to content management. The same protocol can be used to integrate with databases, APIs, file systems, and virtually any other type of application or service.

For content editors, this means being able to use natural language to perform complex tasks. Instead of navigating through a complex admin interface, you can simply ask your AI assistant to "create a new blog post about MCP with an image of a network" and it will handle all the details.

MCP servers are the future of AI application integration.
