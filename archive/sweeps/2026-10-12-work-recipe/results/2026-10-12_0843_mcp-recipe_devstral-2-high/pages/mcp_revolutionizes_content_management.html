MCP Revolutionizes Content Management

Published on October 12, 2026

Discover how MCP servers transform the way we manage content in Umbraco and other CMS platforms.

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is changing how developers and content editors interact with CMS platforms. Gone are the days of complex APIs and custom integrations.

MCP provides a standardized interface that AI agents can use to perform virtually any content management task, from creating simple pages to managing complex media libraries.

What makes MCP truly revolutionary is its simplicity. Instead of writing custom code for each integration, you define a set of tools that your application exposes. AI agents can then discover and use these tools through a standardized protocol.

This means that once you've set up your MCP server for Umbraco, any AI assistant that supports MCP can immediately start managing your content without any additional configuration.

MCP is the missing link between AI and content management systems.
