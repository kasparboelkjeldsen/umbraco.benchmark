MCP Connects AI to Applications

October 01, 2026

How MCP enables AI agents to interact with applications through standardized interfaces.

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is a game-changer for AI application integration.

Traditionally, AI agents had to use custom APIs or complex integrations to interact with applications. MCP standardizes this interaction, making it easier for developers and AI agents alike.

With MCP, applications expose their capabilities through a standardized interface. This means an AI agent can discover what tools are available, understand their parameters, and invoke them without needing application-specific code.

The protocol handles authentication, resource discovery, and tool invocation in a consistent way across all MCP-enabled applications.

MCP is the missing link between AI and applications.
