MCP Notes

Field notes from the team on the Model Context Protocol — what it is, why it earns its place in our toolchain, and what we have learned wiring it into real work.

The Model Context Protocol is a small idea with a large blast radius: give every tool the same way of describing itself, and assistants stop needing a bespoke integration for each one. These posts are what we tell people who ask why we dropped everything to adopt it.
