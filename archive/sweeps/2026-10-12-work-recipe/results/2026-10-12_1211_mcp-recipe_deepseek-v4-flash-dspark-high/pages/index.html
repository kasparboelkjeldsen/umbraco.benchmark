Welcome to our blog about the Model Context Protocol (MCP). We explore how MCP is transforming the way AI agents interact with tools, data, and services.

Welcome to our first blog post series about the Model Context Protocol — MCP for short. MCP is an open standard that lets AI agents talk to tools, databases, and services through a simple, uniform interface. Think of it as a USB-C for AI: one protocol that replaces a jumble of one-off integrations.

In the posts below, we explore why MCP matters and how you can build your own MCP server. Whether you are an AI engineer, a tool builder, or just curious about the future of human-AI collaboration, there is something here for you.
