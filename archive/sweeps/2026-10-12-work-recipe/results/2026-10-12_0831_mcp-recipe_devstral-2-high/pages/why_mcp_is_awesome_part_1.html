Why MCP is Awesome - Part 1

October 12, 2026

MCP provides a powerful way to interact with Umbraco through a standardized interface.

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is a revolutionary way to interact with applications like Umbraco. It provides a standardized interface that allows external tools and agents to perform operations without needing to understand the underlying implementation details.

In this first post, I'll explain the basics of MCP and why it's such a game-changer for content management systems.

With MCP, you can perform complex operations through a simple, standardized interface. This means that tools like Claude Code can help you manage your Umbraco site without needing to understand the specifics of Umbraco's API.

The protocol abstracts away the complexity, making it easier to integrate with various systems.

MCP is the future of application integration. It simplifies complex workflows and makes automation accessible to everyone.
