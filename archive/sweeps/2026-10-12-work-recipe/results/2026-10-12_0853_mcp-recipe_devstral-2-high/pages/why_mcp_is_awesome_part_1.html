Why MCP is Awesome - Part 1

Published on October 01, 2026

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is a game-changer for AI agent development. It provides a standardized interface that allows AI models to discover, understand, and interact with tools and resources in a consistent way.

Before MCP, each AI application had to implement its own tooling infrastructure. This led to fragmentation and made it difficult to share tools between different agents.

With MCP, tools can be defined once and used by any compliant AI agent. This standardization reduces development time and increases reliability.

MCP servers expose capabilities through a well-defined schema, making it easy for agents to discover what tools are available and how to use them.

MCP is the foundation for the next generation of AI-powered applications.
