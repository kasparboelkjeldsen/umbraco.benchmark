<p>Model Context Protocol (MCP) represents a paradigm shift in how AI systems interact with external tools and data. At its core, MCP provides a standardized interface that allows language models to seamlessly access external resources without proprietary integrations.</p>
<p><strong>Key benefits of MCP:</strong> Reduces vendor lock-in, enables consistent tool integration across different AI platforms, and provides developers with a cleaner architecture for building AI applications.</p>
<p>By using MCP, developers can focus on their core business logic while the protocol handles the complexity of tool integration. This leads to more maintainable, flexible, and scalable AI applications.</p>
